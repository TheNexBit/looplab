For guitarists and musicians who learn by ear

Slow it down, loop it, nail the part.

Looplab is the practice lab for your songs and lessons: millisecond-precise loops, independent speed and pitch, synced tabs, the solo and the backing track one above the other. Free, on your PC, no account.

Features

Everything you need to learn a song, in one window

Built like a DAW: lit buttons, knobs, shortcuts and pedals. Nothing to type while you play.

Precise loops

Drag across the waveform, add a lead-in before the loop and a gap between repeats. Save your loops and line them up.

lead-in 0–10 s · gap 0–10 s

Speed and pitch

Slow down without changing the pitch, or transpose without changing the tempo. The speed trainer raises the speed for you.

speed 25–200% · ±12 st and ±50 cents

Synced tabs

Open Guitar Pro, MusicXML or PDF next to the song: the bar that plays lights up, and a click on the tab moves the music.

Guitar Pro · MusicXML · PDF

Solo and backing together

Open the solo and its backing track as stems, lined up by their sound. «Listen» switches between them without stopping the music.

up to 9 lanes · S · M · volume

Stem separation

Split vocals, guitar, bass, drums and piano out of any song and set each one with its own fader.

6 stems · processed on your PC

Setlist & Live mode

Build the setlist for your gig, rehearse the changeovers and play the set on the full-screen Stage view, pedal included.

gap between songs · segue · expected end

All your courses in one place

Add a course folder: every lesson opens ready to go, with video, camera angles, backing track and tab.

done · to review · pick up where you left off

A serious metronome

A click that follows the song's tempo map, count-in, subdivisions, accents and rhythm drills, or on its own.

20–400 BPM · custom sounds

Everything stays yours

Works offline. Loops, markers and notes live in a library on your PC, with automatic backups.

no account · no subscription

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free. Donations on Ko-fi are optional and unlock nothing.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Looplab runs on your PC; your loops, markers and notes stay in a local library with automatic backups.

Can I use my own video courses?

Yes. Add the course folder: Looplab recognises lessons, camera angles, backing tracks and tabs and opens them together.

Does it run on Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. Other platforms are being considered.

Can I control it with a pedal?

Yes: MIDI pedals can be assigned to any command, and page-turner pedals (Page Down/Page Up) work in the Setlist.

Is the app in English?

The interface is in Italian today; an English version is on the way.
